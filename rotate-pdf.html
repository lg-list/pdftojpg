Fix scanned PDF orientation

Scanned PDFs often contain pages that are sideways or upside down. This tool lets you rotate all pages or only selected pages, then save a corrected copy for reading, printing, or sharing.

Best uses

  • Fix sideways scans from office copiers.
  • Turn upside down forms before printing.
  • Rotate only a few pages in a mixed-orientation PDF.

Related tools

Split PDFExtract corrected pages into a smaller file. Merge PDFCombine corrected PDFs into one document.

When to rotate PDF pages

Rotate PDF is built for sideways scans, upside down pages, landscape forms, and pages captured from a phone at the wrong angle. Fixing orientation before sharing makes the document easier to read and print.

Review before saving

Preview the selected rotation and make sure every page faces the right direction before downloading. The browser applies the rotation without uploading the file.
