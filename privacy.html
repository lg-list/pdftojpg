Privacy

Privacy Policy

PDF Tools is built around browser-based PDF and image processing. Selected files are handled locally by your browser for the tools on this site.

Local file processingNo account requiredTransparent third-party services

File processing

When you choose a PDF or image, the file is read by scripts running in your browser. The conversion, merge, split, rotation, watermarking, numbering, compression, resize, or format conversion steps are performed locally on your device. The site does not require an account and does not provide a server upload step for these tools.

Browser storage

Tools may create temporary in-browser object URLs so you can download generated files. These temporary URLs are created during the session and are released by the page after the download flow completes.

Third-party services

The site may load third-party scripts, including Google AdSense, to show ads or support site operations. Those services may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies according to their own policies.

What you control

You can avoid selecting sensitive files, close the browser tab after using a tool, clear browser data, or use browser privacy settings and ad controls offered by your browser or ad providers.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The goal is to keep the file handling model clear: document and image transformations happen in your browser, while third-party scripts are disclosed separately.
